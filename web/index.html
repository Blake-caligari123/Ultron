Why bots get cut

1

Bred

Ultron creates thousands of trading bots, each with its own simple rule. Many rules combine 1 to 3 indicators (RSI, MACD, EMA and SMA crosses, Bollinger, Keltner, ATR, ADX, Stochastic, a time-weighted VWAP, Supertrend, Ichimoku, Donchian, daily pivots) joined by AND or OR. Children swap and tweak whole indicator conditions. Every bot aims for 2 to 3 times what it risks (reward:risk 1:2 to 1:3). No more than 2 bots on the floor (3 in the Scalp Pit) may use the same indicator combination. The best bots have children; the worst are deleted.

2

Tested

Survivors must make money, after realistic trading costs, on price history they never saw while being bred, and keep working when their settings are nudged slightly. Most bots fail here.

3

Paper trading

The bots that pass trade live gold and bitcoin prices with pretend money. No broker, no real orders. A central risk manager caps the total risk in use, the number of open trades and the trades in one direction, and stops all new trades for the day if the floor loses too much.

4

Learns nonstop

Ultron never stops. Around the clock it breeds and tests new bots on older prices, then checks the best ones on the last 6 months they never trained on. Passers join the floor straight away as trial bots with a small amount of money (at most 3 new bots an hour). Bots that lose too much are cut on the spot, and the money is re-shared every time the team changes.

⚡

Scalp Pit (experimental)

A second, fully separate floor with its own $10,000 paper account, risk limits and breeder. Its bots scalp on the 1-minute chart around bigger-picture price levels: the last completed 1-hour and 4-hour high and low, yesterday's high and low, the Asia and London session high and low, and round numbers. They either buy the retest after a level breaks, fade a false break (a wick through the level that closes back inside), or buy the first pullback after a break. The stop sits just beyond the level or wick and the target is 2 to 3 times the risk. Trading costs hit hard at this speed, so a bot must still beat 75% of random bots on the unseen 6 months to get in.
